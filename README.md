# Confidently Wrong

A party quiz show for one big screen. Play it at **https://slimshadi20.github.io/confidently-wrong/**

- Open the link on the computer plugged into the TV (Microsoft Edge has the best announcer voices), press **Start the show**.
- Everyone scans the QR code on the TV with their phone: players get a buzzer, the host sees the answers.
- No accounts, no installs. Keyboard buzzers (Q P Z M A L T V) work too.

Two rounds of buzz-in trivia, hidden Send It and Chaos tiles, house rules, Final Answer bets and the Hive Mind survey round. Up to 8 players, or teams of up to 12.
